# Lesson alerts

This lab shows the five GitHub alert types a lesson can use: note, tip, important, warning, and caution.

## The five alerts

> [!NOTE]
> A note adds something useful to know, even when skimming.

> [!TIP]
> A tip shows a better or easier way to do something.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Important marks what the learner needs to know to finish the step.

> [!WARNING]
> A warning points out a problem the learner should avoid right now.

> [!CAUTION]
> Caution warns that an action can have a bad result.

## Summary

- Start a quote with `[!NOTE]`, `[!TIP]`, `[!IMPORTANT]`, `[!WARNING]`, or `[!CAUTION]` on its own line.
- The rest of the quote is the alert's text.
